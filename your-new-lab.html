What is the model actually doing?

This classroom model uses numeric features (width and height) and labeled samples. It is not image recognition and needs no neural-network training.

distance = √((width − sample width)² + (height − sample height)²)

Sort by distance and let every sample at or inside the kth distance vote once. With fewer than k samples, use all of them. Equal vote counts produce Tie. With no samples, add one before predicting.

The model follows your labels even if a label seems geometrically wrong. It never replaces the neighbor calculation with a width-versus-height rule.
